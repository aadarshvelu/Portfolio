# The Upgrade — Flow

## Entry

The hero scroll reaches its final beat — confetti bursts from the cone.
Among the confetti, a film frame tumbles out. It spins once, growing as
it flies toward the viewer, and locks full-screen as the confetti settles.

That film frame IS the upgrade page. As it flies in you can already see
the title, polaroids, clapperboard, and chrome printed on the celluloid.

---

## Title Slate

The frame lands. A deep navy night sky fills the screen — stars twinkle,
slow shooting stars drift diagonally across.

Centre screen:

> **THE · UPGRADE**
> *after the first light*

Sprocket strips run along the top and bottom edges. Letterbox bars frame
the image. A clapperboard sits top-left. Film chrome (reel number, scene,
beat count) lines the margins. Registration marks anchor the four corners.

A scroll hint pulses at the bottom: *SCROLL · ENTER THE SCENE*.

Three polaroid photographs are scattered in the lower-right — Kaggle,
Kozhikode, AWS — each with a handwritten caption beneath.

---

## Zoom — Kaggle

Scrolling zooms the camera toward the Kaggle polaroid (bottom-right,
tilted slightly). The title fades out as the zoom commits. The polaroid
grows until its photo cell dominates the screen.

The placeholder text ("2020 / KAGGLE") crossfades into the voice-over
card — a screenplay-style insert:

> *AI was booming. I went the other way — foundations first.
> Math. Stats. The slow parts. Practiced on Kaggle until it clicked.*

Credential line: **Kaggle — 3× Expert**, 2022 — 2025.

Caption fades back in: *first run*.

---

## Pan — Kozhikode

The camera pans right and slightly up, settling on the Kozhikode
polaroid (tilted the other way). Same crossfade — placeholder dissolves,
voice-over card appears:

> *AI writes the code now. As a dev — that's not enough.
> I need to think in business cycles. Pitch to market.*

Credential line: **IIM Kozhikode** — Executive Alumni, 2024.

Caption: *the upgrade.*

---

## Pan — AWS

A final pan upward to the AWS polaroid (smaller, subtle tilt). Same
reveal:

> *Final year of college. Got certified. Aced cloud.
> Knew the infrastructure before I needed it.*

Credential line: **AWS — SAA**, Solutions Architect, Feb 2023.

Caption: *p.s.*

---

## Throughout

- Night sky, twinkling stars, and shooting stars persist behind everything.
- Film grain, vignette, and scanlines overlay the entire page.
- Sprockets and letterbox bars stay fixed at the edges — they never zoom.
- Each polaroid's voice-over card is tiny at base scale, designed to read
  clearly only once the zoom brings it close.
- Scrolling back reverses everything — zoom rewinds, pans retrace, the
  title reappears, and the film frame eventually tumbles back into the
  confetti.
